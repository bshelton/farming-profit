# Farming Profit artwork

Original artwork generated with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. Transparent PNGs are bundled locally; the plugin does not download artwork.

## Assets

- `mark-master.png`: larger sprouting-coin mark for future exports.
- `harvest-master.png`: larger harvest illustration for future exports.
- `../../icon.png`: 48 × 48 listing icon.
- `../../src/main/resources/com/farmingprofit/brand/mark-24.png`: 24 × 24 navigation and header icon.
- `../../src/main/resources/com/farmingprofit/brand/harvest-144.png`: 144 × 80 empty-state illustration.

Exports were trimmed and resized with ImageMagick, preserving transparency. The illustration appears only when there is no active run, keeping the active tracker compact.

## Generation prompts

### Mark

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original compact game-plugin emblem for 'Farming Profit', a RuneLite farming-profit tracker. Subject: one warm golden coin with a two-leaf green herb sprout growing from its top, visually unified silhouette. Style: crisp charming old-school fantasy inventory icon, hand-painted pixel-art-inspired chunky shapes, very limited detail, dark brown outline, muted moss green and warm gold highlights. Must remain legible reduced to 24x24 pixels. Centered single isolated emblem filling 80 percent of square composition. Genuine transparent background. No lettering, no numbers, no text, no border frame, no glow, no shadows outside the emblem. Original design, not a copy of RuneScape artwork.

### Harvest illustration

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a small original UI spot illustration for a RuneLite farming-profit plugin, no text. Subject: a low rustic wooden harvest crate containing a few leafy green herbs, with three warm gold coins leaning against one corner and a tiny two-leaf sprout nearby. Restrained old-school fantasy inventory-art aesthetic, hand-painted pixel-art-inspired chunky forms, brown outlines, muted moss greens and warm golden highlights. Simple clear silhouettes with few details, readable at 144 pixels wide by 80 pixels high. One compact landscape arrangement centered, no ground plane or scenery. Genuine transparent background, no text, no lettering, no UI, no border frame, no glow. Original artwork, not a copy of existing RuneScape assets.
